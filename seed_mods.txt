 --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
|                  This file lists the numbers added to the seed in order for specific generations.                  |
| This is solely to ensure that the same seed is not used for two different spawns (such as a structure and an ore). |
|                              Irrational numbers are used to prevent overlapping seeds.                             |
 --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Default seed                       | seed

Temperature                        | seed + Math.LN10
Humidity                           | seed + Math.LN10 * 2
Biome Seed Modifiers               | seed + index / (7.8 + Math.E)

Heightmap                          | seed + Math.sqrt(Math.E)
Large Heightmap                    | seed + Math.sqrt(Math.E) * 2
Mountain Heightmap                 | seed + Math.sqrt(Math.E) * 3
Large Mountain Heightmap           | seed + Math.sqrt(Math.E) * 4

Normal Caves                       | seed + Math.E
Small Caves                        | seed + Math.E * 2
Dripstone                          | seed + Math.E * 3
Dripstone End                      | seed + Math.E * 4

Ores                               | seed + Math.cbrt(3) (+ Math.SQRT1_2)
Crystals                           | above + 0.1
Structures                         | seed (+ Math.E * Math.PI for offset)

Veins                              | seed + Math.sqrt(Math.PI)
Geodes                             | seed + Math.sqrt(Math.PI) * 2

Deep Space                         | seed + Math.PI * 2
Intertemperate                     | seed + Math.sqrt(11, 13) (noise, and x/y/z offset respectively)
Basement                           | seed + Math.sqrt(2, 3, 5, 7) (all used for x, y, z, and perlin noise respectively)
